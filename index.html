The Culprit

How to Play

Goal

Every round, a crime has been committed. The innocent must figure out who the culprit(s) are before they outruns you!

Setup

  • Enter your crew name and how many players are playing (4-10).
  • Each player enters their name one by one.
  • Each player gets a secret card with their role. Only they can see it.
  • Culprits know they are guilty. Innocents don't know who is guilty.

Number of Culprits

  • For 4 players: 1 culprit
  • For 5-6 players: 2 culprit
  • For 7-9 players: 3 culprit
  • For 10 players: 4 culprit

Gameplay

  • Introduce Yourselves: Each player says their job role, but culprits shouldn't say that they are guilty.
  • The crime: Find out what terrible thing happened.
  • Discussion: Talk and share your suspicions.
  • Clue: A clue is revealed after each vote round, the clues in the rounds after the third round is repeated.
  • Vote: Everyone votes for who they suspect. Highest vote gets eleminated.

Winning

  • Innocents win when all culprits are eliminated.
  • Culprits win when they equal or outnumber the innocents remaining.

Tiebreaker

If votes are tied, the group must agree on someone to eliminate (rock-paper-scissors, Flip a coin, argue louder - dealer's choice).

Tips

  • Watch body language and inconsistencies in stories.
  • Culprits: stay calm, deflect suspicion, blend in.
  • Innocents: use clues wisely, trust your gut.
  • Swearing is strictly prohibited in the game.
