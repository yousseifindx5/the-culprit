The Culprit

Can you find the guilty one?
The culprit is an HTML game made for groups to play, it needs from 4 to 10 players to play it. A crime has been committed, everyone gets a role, one or more players are the culprits. Culprits have to hide between the innocents and try to convince them that they are innocents.

How to play The Culprit?
open index.html, press how to play button, setup your crew name, player names, get your role and try to find the culprits or hide if you are a culprit.

Files:
1-index.html
2-style.css
3-script.js
No build step, no dependencies, no server requiredو just open index.html in a browser.

Project Status:
Fully playable end-to-end: setup, role reveal, introductions, crime reveal, discussion, cumulative clues, voting (with tie-breaking), win/lose conditions, and the full story reveal.
